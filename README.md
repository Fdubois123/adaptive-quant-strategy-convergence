# Adaptive Quantitative Strategy Convergence

An institutional-style quantitative intelligence and portfolio
research platform.

## Core Objectives

- Multi-regime financial market detection
- Adaptive quantitative strategy convergence
- Global macroeconomic intelligence
- Geopolitical and climate risk intelligence
- Probabilistic market-stress early warning
- Portfolio digital twin
- Portfolio and strategy risk analytics
- Scenario and stress testing
- Explainable quantitative intelligence
- Advanced performance analytics
- Interactive institutional-style quant terminal

## Quantitative Foundation

The platform is designed to integrate the existing
Algorithmic Trading Engine v1.0 as its quantitative research core.
